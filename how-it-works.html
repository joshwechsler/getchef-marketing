02

Upload the PDF to ChefOS.

Drop in the file and let ChefOS parse it in about 30 seconds. Review the import — recipe count, meal count, any flagged rows — before sending work to stations. Nothing fires until you confirm.

03

Recipes auto-sort to stations.

Category and prefix rules route work to Protein, Cold Prep, Sauce, Packing, and more. Every cook sees only their list. Nobody hunts for their page or cross-checks another station's column.

04

Staff log in and get to work.

PIN login gets each cook into the right station view in seconds. Recipes move from not started to in progress to complete. No shared logins, no wrong views, no time lost navigating.

05

Managers watch production move.

Progress bars, shortage alerts, and station status update live on the manager dashboard. The floor is visible without leaving the screen. One glance replaces ten check-in laps.

06

Pack with confidence.

Meal sizes sit side by side. Color-coded scoop cues guide the line. Every meal gets checked off before it leaves the pass. No more reading off a laminated spec sheet mid-rush.
