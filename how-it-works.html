06

Meal sizes sit side by side. Color-coded scoop cues guide the line. Allergen badges surface immediately so no meal ships without the right warnings. The manager dashboard shows food cost per cover in real time. Every meal gets checked off before it leaves the pass.

Count inventory, get manager approval, generate orders from your guide, and receive goods against a PO — all in one system.

Start a structured count session for a location. Fill quantities line by line as you walk the shelves. ChefOS saves your progress automatically — pick up where you left off if you're interrupted.

02

Manager reviews and approves.

Complete the count and submit for review. The manager sees variance against par for every line. If something looks off, they can unlock the count for correction. Once approved, the snapshot is locked to your records.

03

Generate your order.

From your order guide, ChefOS auto-suggests what to order based on inventory gaps versus par. Or generate a purchase plan directly from your next production event — what you need to produce the run, minus what you have on hand.

04

Receive and reconcile.

PO is sent to the supplier. When goods arrive, record received quantities per line. ChefOS flags variances between ordered and received — so you know exactly what came in short before production starts.

Track 3

02

Auto-populate at print time.

Select a recipe, enter the production date — ChefOS auto-fills nutrition facts, the ingredient statement ordered descending by weight, allergen declarations, and the best-by date calculated from shelf-life days set on the recipe. No copy-paste, no manual lookup.

03

Queue and print.

Send a batch print job to your label printer. Each job is tracked by ID — you can see queue status, reprint a job, or check what was printed and when. Compatible with standard thermal label printers.

Track 4

Reporting & AI

Ask questions in plain English, get grounded answers with source citations, and pull structured reports — all from your live kitchen data.

01

Ask anything.

Type a question in plain English. The AI searches your live recipe and ingredient data — not generic knowledge. Ask about food cost impact, ingredient substitutions, margin on specific meals, or anything tied to your actual numbers.

02

Grounded answers with sources.

Responses stream in with source citations — which recipes, which pricing records, which production numbers the answer drew from. No hallucinated numbers. Every figure is traceable back to your data.
