Questions kitchens ask first.

Everything you need to know before the demo call.

How long does setup take?

Most teams review a first production import in 30 minutes. Station routing and prefix rules are tuned during onboarding so work goes to the right station from day one.

Do we need special tablets?

No. ChefOS runs in a modern browser on common kitchen tablets, laptops, and manager screens. No app install, no special hardware.

Does it work with Parsley?

Yes. ChefOS supports Parsley Sub-Recipe Summary PDF import out of the box. Export from Parsley, upload to ChefOS, and your station work is ready to review in under a minute.

Can staff use PIN login?

Yes. Staff PINs keep login fast during a busy shift while preserving role-based access. Cooks see only their station. Managers see everything.

Is our production data secure?

ChefOS is designed around role-based access with controlled production data visibility. Each user sees only the data their role requires.

Can you build our integration?

Yes. Share your export format or API details and we can scope a custom integration during the onboarding process. ChefTec and others are in development.
